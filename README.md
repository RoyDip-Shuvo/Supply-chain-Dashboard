# StockUp Supply Chain Analytics Dashboard

## Overview
This project showcases a Power BI Supply Chain Dashboard designed to provide critical insights into the supply chain performance for the company StockUp. The dashboard consolidates multiple key performance indicators (KPIs), allowing stakeholders to assess revenue, costs, defect rates, stock levels, and lead times efficiently.

## Key Features:
**Revenue & Cost Analysis:**
Displays overall revenue, manufacturing cost, shipping cost, and gross profit for the business.

**Defect Rate Tracking:**
Visualizes defect percentages and helps in assessing product quality across different product types (haircare, skincare, cosmetics).

**Stock Level & Production Volume Monitoring:**
Shows the total salable stock and production volume to track inventory health.

**Lead Time Performance:**
A series of gauges visualizing lead time metrics such as lead time performance, order lead times, manufacturing lead times, and shipping time.

**Supply Chain Risk Assessment:**
A bar chart indicating the risk factor for different SKUs (Stock Keeping Units), with visual indicators (Red, Orange, Green) for immediate action, monitoring, or satisfactory status.

**Cost-Benefit Analysis of Transportation Modes:**
Analyzes the costs of various transportation methods (Road, Rail, Sea, Air) to optimize logistics strategies.

**Defect Rate Comparison Among Suppliers:**
A comparative analysis of defect rates across different suppliers and product categories, highlighting areas for potential quality improvements.



## Filters & Customization:
The dashboard includes dynamic slicers for

**Location:** View data for different regions.

**Supplier Name:** Filter data for specific suppliers.

**Shipping Carriers:** Analyze performance across various shipping carriers.

**Inspection Results:** Filter by inspection outcomes.

**Routes:** Compare data across different shipping routes.

## Technology Stack

**Power BI:** Dashboard creation and data visualization.

**DAX:** Utilized for dynamic calculations and metrics.

**UX Design:** Applied principles of user experience to ensure ease of use, interactivity, and actionable insights.


## Project Goals:
The aim of this dashboard is to

- Provide a comprehensive view of the company's supply chain metrics.
- Enable decision-makers to identify and act on key issues such as high defect rates, slow lead times, and high transportation costs.
- Support continuous improvement by enabling comparison of suppliers, products, and transportation methods.
